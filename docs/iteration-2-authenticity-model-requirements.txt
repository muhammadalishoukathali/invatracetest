INVATRACE — DEFERRED PHOTO-AUTHENTICITY MODEL RESEARCH
Target: Iteration 2 or later

Scope
-----
Iteration 1 E2 uses deterministic automated rules. It does not claim to detect
screen recaptures, printed photographs, edited composites, or other sophisticated
fake evidence. No human coordinator reviews ordinary reports.

A future authenticity model may supplement the rules only after an evaluated
production artifact exists. Until then, the public API must continue returning
authenticityAssessed=false for rule-screened records.

Desired future output
---------------------
- authenticity risk calibrated for genuine field-camera photos versus screen
  recaptures, printed-photo recaptures, edited composites, and non-camera images;
- reason categories suitable for a private audit record;
- immutable model and preprocessing versions; and
- an explicit unavailable/error outcome that never publishes a report.

Evidence required before implementation
---------------------------------------
- Malaysian field-phone evaluation data separated by device, location,
  contributor, and plant instance from training data;
- screen and print recaptures covering multiple displays, printers, lighting
  conditions, viewing angles, moire patterns, glare, and camera devices;
- precision, recall, AUROC/AUPRC, false-accept, and genuine-photo false-reject
  rates at the proposed operating threshold;
- breakdowns by device, image resolution, habitat, and lighting;
- training-data provenance, licences, privacy/consent handling, limitations, and
  representative failure cases; and
- CPU runtime, memory, timeout, and deterministic golden-test results in the
  production Python container.

Integration constraints
-----------------------
- No external inference API.
- The supplied browser E1 model and its class order remain unchanged.
- Deterministic replay, quality, GPS, rate-limit, and time/location rules remain
  independent hard gates and cannot be bypassed by a model score.
- Model failure, timeout, invalid output, or missing artifacts remain fail-closed.
- Do not enable or advertise authenticity assessment until the evaluation and
  operational acceptance criteria are satisfied.
