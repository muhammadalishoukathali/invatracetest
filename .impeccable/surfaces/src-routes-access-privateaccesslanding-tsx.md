Scope: the first-run, recovery handoff, restore, and authenticated access-management routes. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: a field contributor establishing a pseudonymous profile, safeguarding recovery, restoring it on another device, or managing authorized installations. The primary action is deliberate private entry without conventional registration.

Direction: extend InvaTrace's quiet daylight field-utility world as a private field pass. The landing pairs a factual promise with one connected access trail; the recovery-code batch is the memorable focal moment; management reads as an equipment ledger, not a dashboard.

Constraints: no email/password language, raw codes in memory only, public ID and secret codes visually distinct, mobile first, 44px targets, focus-managed headings, live status, reduced motion, generic restore failure, and no accidental offline profile creation.

Unresolved: production backend hashing, transactional consumption, distributed rate limiting, CSRF transport, and database migrations remain outside this repository.
